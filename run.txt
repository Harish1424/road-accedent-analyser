🚗 Road Accident Analysis & Predictive Risk System
The Road Accident Analysis & Predictive Risk System is an end-to-end Python Data Science and Web Application built with Streamlit, Scikit-Learn, Folium, Plotly, and SQLite.

It helps traffic authorities, emergency responders, and urban planners monitor road accident logs, identify high-risk geospatial hotspots, predict accident risk levels under varying weather/road conditions, and deploy targeted safety interventions.

🧠 Data Science & Machine Learning Pipeline
1. Composite Severity Scoring
Each incident record is assigned a quantitative severity metric based on human impact and monetary damages:

$$\text{Severity Score} = (\text{Fatalities} \times 10) + (\text{Injuries} \times 4) + \left(\frac{\text{Material_Loss_Cost}}{10000}\right)$$

2. Geospatial Risk Clustering (Unsupervised K-Means)
Features: Latitude, Longitude, Severity_Score
Algorithm: KMeans(n_clusters=3) weighted by severity score.
Risk Categorization: Clusters are mapped to human-readable risk levels by sorting cluster mean severity scores:
Low Risk (🟢): Lowest mean severity cluster
Medium Risk (🟡): Moderate mean severity cluster
High Risk (🔴): Highest mean severity cluster
3. Predictive Modeling (Supervised Random Forest Classifier)
Features: Hour_of_Day (0–23), Day_of_Week, Road_Type, Weather_Condition
Target: Risk_Level (High, Medium, Low)
Pipeline: One-Hot Encoding for categorical variables + Random Forest Classifier (100 estimators).
Function: Predicts risk likelihood for any custom scenario inputs.
4. Actionable Recommendation Engine
Based on the predicted risk level and environmental factors, the engine automatically outputs targeted interventions:

High Risk (🔴): Dispatch highway patrol units, setup radar speed traps, position ambulances at 5km radii, activate signal priority overrides.
Medium Risk (🟡): Increase stationary police presence, activate variable message signs, check street lighting.
Low Risk (🟢): Maintain baseline CCTV surveillance and standard patrol shifts.
Weather Advisories: Wet pavement speed reductions (Rain/Storm), high-intensity fog beacons (Fog), ramp metering (Expressways).
🖥️ Core Dashboard Features
📍 Registered Places & Risk Status:

Monitored locations table displaying Risk Level badges, Severity Scores, Fatalities, Injuries, and Loss Costs.
Live search bar to filter locations by name or risk level.
High-Risk Hotspots Alert banner highlighting places requiring urgent attention.
➕ Add New Location Record:

Form to log new accident entries (Date, Time, Location_Name, Latitude, Longitude, Injuries, Fatalities, Loss Cost, Road Type, Weather).
Automatically saves entries to SQLite DB and dynamically re-clusters risk levels across all places.
🤖 Risk Level Predictor:

Scenario input sliders & dropdowns to evaluate risk levels for custom times, days, road types, and weather.
Displays model prediction probabilities and recommended action plans.
🗺️ Geospatial Map View:

Dual map engine: Folium Interactive Map (with density heatmaps and colored circle markers) and Plotly Geospatial Map.
📊 Analytics & Trends:

Plotly analytical charts: Hourly accident frequency (24-hour cycle), cumulative severity trends over time, injuries vs fatalities by location, and weather condition breakdown.
